About This Model

How Beyond the Arc DL turns a forecast into a bet

← Back to today's predictions

What this is

Beyond the Arc DL is a college basketball forecasting and betting system. Two neural networks produce a score forecast for every Division I game from pregame information only. A separate decision layer then compares that forecast with the market and decides whether a bet has value, on which side, and how much to stake. The forecast never sees the betting line. The line enters exactly once, as an input to the decision, so the model is always betting against the market rather than blending with it.

The workflow

The forecast

  • Inputs are strictly pregame. Season-to-date team statistics, roster context, rest, site and the point in the season, built so that nothing from the game itself or from later games can leak in.
  • An IC hedge model adjusts each team's season-to-date profile toward what the matchup implies, with bounded moves. It predicts the inputs the game will be played with, not the score.
  • An ensemble of ten thousand members is generated around those hedged profiles, after a recent-tempo adjustment, within each team's recent variability. A frozen scoring model scores every member once. The ensemble's mean is the forecast on the sheet and its shares are the probabilities; there is no separate point forecast.
  • Calibration and pregame adjustments correct the score level through the season and account for who sat a team's last game and for postseason back-to-backs.

The decision

  • Spreads. A fitted cover model turns the gap between our margin and the spread into a probability of covering, and a bet is placed only above a cutoff. A gradient-boosted second layer runs beside it and the two are compared daily.
  • Totals. Bets are placed on the under only, in the specific game profile where the forecast has beaten the line across multiple seasons. Overs are shown but never staked.
  • Moneylines. The model's win probability is pulled part of the way toward the market's own probability before any edge is counted, so only disagreements the model is entitled to keep become bets.
  • Sizing. Fractional Kelly on the calibrated probability, with per-bet, per-game and daily caps. A confidence score from 0 to 100 accompanies every bet: 0 is break-even at the price, 100 is the best hit rate the evidence supports.

How it is kept honest

  • Every rule and every coefficient was validated on seasons the model had not seen, against closing lines, before it was allowed to stake anything.
  • Every signal on the sheet, staked or not, is graded the next morning against the final score and the closing line. The season record page is generated from that ledger, not by hand.
  • Candidate layers earn promotion only by beating the staked rule on live lines over a season, never on a backtest alone.

Specific thresholds, coefficients, feature definitions and model files are not published.
